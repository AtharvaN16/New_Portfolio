Entry sequence concept

Plasma state → waterblob settle
The blobs enter in a high-energy "plasma" state — intense rim, compressed shape, rising from below. Over ~2.5 seconds they shed energy and settle into the existing soft watercolor look. Color identity stays yours throughout: rim and plasma are derived from your palette, not borrowed from the video.

What changes in the shader: A uRevealPhase uniform (0→1 over ~2.5s) drives the transition. At phase=0: blobs render with sharp SDF rim from color3, dark compressed interior, intense glow — the "plasma" state. At phase=1: the existing shader runs as-is. Between: a smooth lerp in the color and softness calculations. The uYOffset rise already exists — we're just giving it a dramatic visual costume for the first ~1.5s.
