What changed from v1: The blobs no longer enter as two distinct bright objects. The entry is one large dark form — energy at the boundary, void at the center. The "two blob" identity only emerges during differentiation. This is closer to the physics of the reference video and truer to the idea of something being born rather than something arriving.
