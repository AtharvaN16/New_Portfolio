Exploring direction

Palette-derived rim lighting — how it adapts across your 9 palettes
Dark mode — emissive rim from color3 (pop/accent)
Light mode — pigment accumulation at edges (darker rim, ink metaphor)
How the rim is derived: Each palette has three colors. Color 3 (the rightmost, "pop" accent) becomes the rim light. In dark mode it's additive — a glow radiates outward from the blob boundary. In light mode it inverts — the same hue darkens and concentrates at the edges like pigment pooling, consistent with the existing watercolor metaphor in the shader.
